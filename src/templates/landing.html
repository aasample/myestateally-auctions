Why MyEstateAlly Exists

I experienced every child's worst nightmare—twice. My dad passed away suddenly in March. Just weeks later, in April, I gave birth to my first child. My younger sister also welcomed a premature baby that same month. We should have been celebrating new life, but instead, we watched my mom struggle to manage my dad's estate while grieving.

Then in September, my mom passed away unexpectedly too.

"I was supposed to be experiencing the happiest time of my life. Instead, I was managing two estates, caring for a newborn, working full-time, and trying to support my sister across the country."

The logistics were overwhelming. The paperwork was endless. The decisions were constant. And through it all, I kept thinking: there has to be a better way.

MyEstateAlly was born from that experience. Whether you're closing an estate or simply downsizing, you deserve a simple, private place to organize your thoughts, documents, and belongings—without the fluff, without the sales pitches, and without your data being sold to the highest bidder.

This is the tool I wish I'd had. Now it's here for you.
